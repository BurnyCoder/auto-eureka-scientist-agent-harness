# Final implementation and artifact checks

The accepted plan is implemented as a modular local CLI, with the original policy preserved, selectable provider/model, native unrestricted execution, live search, transactional resume, bounded repair, evidence checks and publication receipts. The [requirement map](../docs/acceptance.md) and [implementation review](implementation-review.md) record the scope and limitations.

The authoritative live outcomes are in [the generated validation report](validation-summary.md). Haiku did not complete its bounded trial. Luna's controller completed its workflow, but publication invalidated evidence hashes and required disclosed mechanical repair afterward. Neither outcome is presented as proof of broad scientific reliability. No additional model inference was used during parent artifact verification.

The [fresh-clone receipt](verification/fresh-clone-reproduction.json) records the tested public harness revision, locked installation, offline test results, unchanged policy hash, numerical regeneration without private workspaces, identical generated figures, and byte comparisons against deployed PDFs and example images. [Haiku](verification/haiku-public-clone.json) and [Luna](verification/luna-public-clone.json) checkpoint receipts record inference from downloaded model bundles. Public phase verification preserves [Haiku's incomplete result](verification/haiku-public-phases.json) and checks [Luna's repaired artifacts](verification/luna-public-phases.json).

Every rendered page of the final [validation paper](verification/validation-paper-visual.json), [Haiku paper](verification/haiku-visual-final.json) and [Luna paper](verification/luna-visual-final.json) was inspected. Explorer interactions checked combined filters, an empty intersection, and restoration of all records. The consolidated hub presents the provenance caveat beside its recorded statuses. Public model bundles, their collections and deployed trial URLs returned successful responses in the saved receipts.

Final post-run publication identifiers are retained for [Haiku](verification/haiku-final-publication.json) and [Luna](verification/luna-final-publication.json). The public repositories contain reproducible artifacts and sanitized summaries; credentials, private run databases and raw transcripts remain excluded. Parent repairs do not add phases to Haiku or change either trial's consumed budgets. The retained failure records are part of the result.
